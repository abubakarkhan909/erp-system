# Al Zahid Jewelry ERP — Client User Guide

**A simple guide for shop owners and staff**  
This document explains what the software does, how to use it day by day, and what every screen means — without technical jargon.

---

## 1. What is this software?

**Al Zahid Jewelry ERP** is a complete shop management system for a **gold and jewelry business in Oman**.

It helps you:

- Sell jewelry and record payments  
- Buy stock from suppliers  
- Track gold by piece and by weight (grams)  
- Manage the cash drawer and bank accounts  
- Follow customer advances and installment plans  
- Calculate **Oman VAT (5%)** and basic accounts  
- Keep a safe backup of your shop data  

**Important:** All your data stays on **your shop computer**. You do not need the internet for daily work. Money is handled in **OMR** (Omani Rial) with three decimal places.

---

## 2. How do you use the app?

### Desktop (recommended for the shop)

1. Install the **Windows Setup** program on the shop PC.  
2. Open **Al Zahid Jewelry ERP** from the desktop or Start menu.  
3. The app opens a shop window. You see the **Login** screen.  
4. Enter your username and password, then click Login.  
5. You arrive at the **Dashboard** — the starting point for the day.

### From another computer on the same network (optional)

If the system is set up for LAN access, staff on another PC can open the same system in a web browser (same Wi‑Fi / network as the shop PC). Ask your installer for the exact address.

### Leaving the app

Use **Logout** when you finish your shift so the next person cannot use your account.

---

## 3. Who can use what? (Roles)

Each staff member has a **role**. The role controls which screens and actions they can use.

| Role | Who it is for | What they can do (simple summary) |
|------|----------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Owner** | Shop owner | Everything: sales, purchases, users, settings, backup restore, cancel (void) invoices, close accounting periods |
| **Manager** | Day-to-day manager | Customers, products, sales, purchases, cash, banks, expenses, reports, create backups — usually **not** users/settings or restore |
| **Cashier** | Counter / till | Serve customers, create and post sales, work with cash, view products and reports |
| **Salesman** | Floor sales | Prepare sales drafts, view customers and products — usually cannot post or cancel invoices |
| **Accountant** | Books & tax | VAT, accounting, expenses, banks, reports — can close a financial period |

> **Tip:** Only the **Owner** should manage users and shop settings. Give cashiers and salesmen only what they need for the counter.

## 4. First-time login and passwords

### Login

1. Open the app.  
2. Enter **Username** and **Password**.  
3. Click Login.

After a fresh install / setup, the system usually has an owner account (your installer will confirm the exact password). **Change the password** after the first login for safety.

### Forgot password

Use **Forgot password** on the login screen. Two options may be available:

1. **Security questions** — answer the questions set for your user, then choose a new password.  
2. **Owner recovery key** — the owner’s secret key (keep this written in a safe place, not on the computer).

### Owner helping staff

The **Owner** can reset a staff password from the **Users** screen.

> Passwords are recovered **inside the shop system** (questions or owner key). There is no email reset — this is intentional for offline shop use.

## 5. The left menu — every screen explained

When you log in, the left side shows the menu. Below is what each item means and when to use it.

---

### 5.1 Dashboard

**Purpose:** See the health of the shop “today” in one place.

You typically see things like:

- Today’s sales, purchases, and expenses  
- Whether the cash drawer session is open  
- Bank totals (high level)  
- Money customers still owe (receivables)  
- VAT for the month so far  
- Low stock warnings  
- Upcoming installment dues  

**How to use it:** Open this first every morning. Act on alerts (low stock, late installments) before the busy hours.

---

### 5.2 Customers

**Purpose:** Keep a list of your customers and how much they owe you.

**What you can do:**

- Add a new customer (name, phone, etc.)  
- Edit or remove a customer  
- See their balance after sales and payments  

**When to use it:** Before selling on credit, or when a regular customer comes in. Always search first so you do not create duplicates.

---

### 5.3 Suppliers

**Purpose:** Keep a list of jewelers / wholesalers you buy from, and what you owe them.

**What you can do:**

- Add / edit / remove suppliers  
- Track payable balances after purchases and payments  

**When to use it:** Before posting a purchase invoice.

---

### 5.4 Products

**Purpose:** Your jewelry catalogue — the items you sell and stock.

Each product can include details such as:

- Name / code  
- Type (for example finished jewelry, gold, stone, service, making)  
- Karat (18, 21, 22, 24)  
- Weight (grams)  
- Making charges / stone charges  
- Selling and cost related information  

**When to use it:** When a new design arrives, or when you correct weight/charges. Sales use these products as line items.

---

### 5.5 Gold Rates

**Purpose:** Enter today’s gold price **per gram** (by karat) in OMR.

**Why it matters:** Sales and valuations often depend on the daily rate. Wrong rates mean wrong invoice totals.

**Daily habit:** Update gold rates every morning before the first sale (or whenever the market rate changes).

---

### 5.6 Sales

**Purpose:** Create customer invoices when you sell jewelry.

**Main screens:**

- **Sales list** — all invoices (draft / posted / void)  
- **New sale** — create a new invoice  

**Typical sale flow:**

1. Open **Sales → New**.  
2. Choose the **customer** (or walk-in, if used).  
3. Add **product lines** (item, quantity, weights, making/stone charges, discounts if needed).  
4. **Save as Draft** if you are still preparing, **or**  
5. **Post** the invoice and record payment (cash, card, bank, cheque, or mixed).  

**What happens when you Post:**

- Stock decreases  
- Customer balance updates (if not fully paid)  
- VAT and accounting entries are recorded  
- Cash / bank movements happen according to the payment  

**Void:** Cancelling a posted invoice (special permission — usually Owner/Manager). Use only for real mistakes; prefer correct procedures instead of voiding casually.

> **Draft vs Posted:** Only **Posted** invoices affect stock, money, and VAT. Drafts are unfinished paperwork.

---

### 5.7 Purchases

**Purpose:** Record stock you buy from suppliers.

**Typical purchase flow:**

1. Open **Purchases → New**.  
2. Choose the **supplier**.  
3. Add lines (products, weight, cost, etc.).  
4. Save draft, or **Post** and optionally pay the supplier (cash/bank).  

**What happens when you Post:**

- Stock increases  
- Supplier payable / cash / bank updates  
- Input VAT is recorded for accounts  

---

### 5.8 Inventory

**Purpose:** See what you have on hand (quantity and/or weight).

Stock changes automatically when you:

- Post purchases (stock up)  
- Post sales (stock down)  
- Process returns / exchanges (when used)  

**Daily habit:** Check Inventory and Dashboard low-stock warnings so you know what to reorder.

---

### 5.9 Cash

**Purpose:** Control the physical cash drawer for the day.

**Typical day:**

1. **Morning — Open session** with opening float (cash in the drawer).  
2. During the day — **Cash in** / **Cash out** for drawer movements, and sales paid in cash link to the till when posted.  
3. **Evening — Close session** — count the cash and compare with what the system expects.  

**Why it matters:** Closing the till daily prevents cash shortages from going unnoticed.

---

### 5.10 Banks

**Purpose:** Record shop bank accounts and movements (deposit, withdraw, transfers).

**When to use it:**

- Customer paid by bank transfer or card settlement  
- You deposit till cash to the bank  
- You pay a supplier from the bank  

Keep bank names and balances accurate so reports and cash-flow make sense.

---

### 5.11 Expenses

**Purpose:** Record shop running costs (electricity, salary, fuel, rent, etc.).

**How it works:**

1. Choose a **category**.  
2. Enter amount, date, and notes.  
3. Choose whether it was paid from **cash** or **bank**.  

This keeps profit reports honest — sales alone do not show the real picture.

---

### 5.12 VAT

**Purpose:** Show Oman VAT for a chosen period (generally **5%**).

**Simple idea:**

- **Output VAT** = VAT you charged customers on sales  
- **Input VAT** = VAT on purchases you can offset  
- Net = what you may need to declare for the period  

**How to use it:**

1. Open **VAT**.  
2. Select the month / period.  
3. Review totals from **posted** documents only.  
4. Export if your accountant needs a file for filing.  
5. When ready, an accountant may **lock** a month so figures stay stable for audit.

---

### 5.13 Accounting

**Purpose:** Financial books and statements for the shop.

You can review:

- Chart of accounts (list of account names used by the system)  
- Trial balance  
- Profit & Loss  
- Balance sheet  
- Cash flow  

Most day-to-day posting (sales, purchases, expenses) **automatically** creates balanced bookkeeping entries. Accountants may also close a **fiscal period** so nobody posts into a finished month by mistake.

> Most shop staff do not need this screen daily. Use Reports and VAT for normal work; leave Accounting to the Owner / Accountant.

---

### 5.14 Reports

**Purpose:** Business summaries for decisions and reviews.

Common reports include:

- Sales performance  
- Inventory valuation  
- Profit overview  
- Expenses  
- Cash flow  
- Installments  
- Advance orders  

**When to use it:** End of day, end of week, or when planning purchases and cash.

---

### 5.15 Advances

**Purpose:** Customer orders where money is paid in advance before delivery (custom piece, booking, etc.).

**Status flow (simple):**

1. **Pending** — order created, waiting  
2. **Ready** — item is ready for customer  
3. **Delivered** — handed over  
4. **Cancelled** — order cancelled  

You can take **advance payments** as the customer pays step by step until delivery.

---

### 5.16 Installments

**Purpose:** Split a sale balance into a payment schedule (monthly / agreed dues).

**Typical flow:**

1. Post a sale that still has an unpaid balance.  
2. Create an **installment plan** (how many payments, first due date, etc.).  
3. When the customer pays a due amount, record the **schedule payment**.  
4. Watch **late** installments on Dashboard / Notifications.

This is ideal for higher-value jewelry sold over several payments.

---

### 5.17 Notifications

**Purpose:** Alerts that need attention, for example:

- Late installment payments  
- Low stock  
- Backup that is getting old  

**Habit:** Open Notifications daily and mark items as read after you handle them.

---

### 5.18 Users

**Purpose:** Create staff logins and assign roles (**Owner only**).

**What the Owner can do:**

- Add a new user  
- Assign role (Manager, Cashier, Salesman, Accountant, etc.)  
- Reset password  
- Set security questions / recovery options  

Never share the Owner password. Each person should have their own login so Audit can show who did what.

---

### 5.19 Settings

**Purpose:** Shop identity printed on documents and used in VAT/invoices.

Typical fields:

- Shop / company name  
- Commercial registration (CR)  
- VAT number  
- Invoice number prefix  
- Receipt footer text  
- Default VAT percentage  

**Change carefully.** Wrong VAT number or shop name on invoices creates problems with customers and tax.

---

### 5.20 Audit

**Purpose:** A history of important actions — who did what, and when.

Use this if something looks wrong (a voided invoice, a changed setting, a deleted master record). Owners and accountants use this for control and trust.

---

### 5.21 Backup

**Purpose:** Protect your shop data against computer failure, theft, or accidental damage.

**Recommended habit:**

1. Create a backup regularly (daily is best for busy shops).  
2. **Download** the backup file to a USB drive or external disk.  
3. Keep at least one copy **off the shop PC**.  

**Restore:** Use only when you must recover an older good copy. After restore, **restart the app**. Restore usually needs Owner (or equivalent) permission.

> Backup is as important as locking the shop door. Without backups, years of sales and balances can disappear if a hard disk fails.

## 6. End-to-end shop flows (follow these)

These flows are the “story” of how the shop runs with the software.

---

### Flow A — Start of day

```
Open app → Login
     → Check Dashboard alerts
     → Update Gold Rates
     → Open Cash session (float)
     → Ready to sell / purchase
```

---

### Flow B — Selling jewelry (most common)

```
Customer arrives
     → Find or create Customer
     → Sales → New invoice
     → Add products / weights / charges
     → Collect payment (cash / card / bank / mixed)
     → Post invoice
     → If balance left → create Installment plan (optional)
     → Hand over jewelry / receipt
```

---

### Flow C — Buying stock

```
Goods arrive from supplier
     → Find or create Supplier
     → Purchases → New
     → Enter items, weights, costs
     → Post (pay now or pay later)
     → Inventory increases automatically
```

---

### Flow D — End of day

```
Review Dashboard / Reports
     → Close Cash session (count drawer)
     → Record bank deposits if any
     → Create Backup → save to USB
     → Logout
```

---

### Flow E — Advance / custom order

```
Customer books an order
     → Advances → Create order
     → Take deposit / later payments
     → Mark Ready when finished
     → Mark Delivered when customer takes it
```

---

### Flow F — Monthly VAT & books (Owner / Accountant)

```
Confirm all invoices are Posted (not left as Draft)
     → Open VAT for the month → review → export if needed
     → Open Accounting reports
     → Close period when month is final (if using this control)
     → Keep backup of that month’s data
```

## 7. Important ideas (learn these once)

### Draft → Posted → Void

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Draft** | Work in progress. Does **not** change stock, cash, or VAT. |
| **Posted** | Final. Changes stock, balances, VAT, and books. |
| **Void** | Cancelled posted document. Used for corrections under strict control. |

### Payments

Sales and purchases can be paid by:

- Cash  
- Bank transfer  
- Card  
- Cheque  
- Mixed (combine methods)  

### Gold business specifics

- Rates are per **gram** and by **karat**  
- A line may include **metal value + making + stones**  
- Inventory often cares about **pieces and grams** together  

### What is “automatic”?

When you post a sale or purchase, the system updates:

- Inventory  
- Customer / supplier balances  
- Cash or bank (if payment is recorded)  
- VAT totals  
- Accounting journals  

You do **not** need to enter books twice.

1. Login  
2. Open cash session

5. Close cash session

2. Maintain products, rates, customers, suppliers  
3. Resolve voids carefully

5. Ensure daily backups are created

1. Review VAT and accounting reports

2. Protect recovery key and backup copies

## 10. Safety rules for the client

1. **Each person uses their own login** — never share passwords.  
2. **Post carefully** — posted means real stock and money movement.  
3. **Backup daily** and keep a copy on USB / second location.  
4. **Update gold rates** before selling.  
5. **Close the cash session** every day.  
6. **Do not restore an old backup** unless you understand you may lose newer work.  
7. **Only Owner** changes Users and Settings.  
8. Keep the **Owner recovery key** written and locked away (not taped to the monitor).

## 12. How the project fits together (for the owner — still simple)

Think of three layers sitting on one shop computer:

1. **Screens** — what staff click (Sales, Cash, VAT…).  
2. **Business rules** — the engine that posts stock, VAT, and books correctly.  
3. **Database file** — the single shop data file protected by **Backup**.  

The **desktop installer** starts everything for you so staff only open one program and log in.

You do **not** need MySQL, XAMPP, or cloud signup for normal shop use.
